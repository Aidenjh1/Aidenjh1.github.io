How to read the result. The formula produces a huge number. Vasta compares only the significand (leading digits) after writing it in scientific notation, ignoring the ×10ⁿ factor. Genesis yields ≈3.14155×10¹⁷ (π ≈ 3.14159); John yields ≈2.71831×10⁴⁰ (e ≈ 2.71828). The Greek text uses Vasta’s orthography with αρχηι (52 letters); without that iota the match to e does not appear.
